# Encouragement Pal

A tiny pal who watches you play and *cheers you on*. Sort of. She is very supportive, in her own deeply sarcastic way.

Pal will speak up when you:
- reach **25%, 50%, 75% and 90%** of a level (by default only milestones you've never reached before)
- die with a **new best** percentage
- **beat** the level

Everything can be toggled in the mod settings, including whether Pal talks in practice mode and how long each message stays on screen.
